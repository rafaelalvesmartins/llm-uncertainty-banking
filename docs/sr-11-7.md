# Mapping `lub` to SR 11-7 (OCC 2011-12)

> **What this page is:** a single, deployer/auditor-facing map of how `lub`'s
> outputs support a model-risk-management program under **SR 11-7 / OCC Bulletin
> 2011-12** ("Supervisory Guidance on Model Risk Management", Federal Reserve &
> OCC, April 2011). It states the guidance's actual structure, shows which `lub`
> artifact evidences each part, and — importantly — says what `lub` does **not**
> do.

## The guidance's actual structure

SR 11-7 is **supervisory guidance** (examiner expectations), not a statute. Its
sections are:

| § | Section | Relevance to `lub` |
|---|---------|--------------------|
| III | **Overview of Model Risk Management** | Introduces **effective challenge** — critical analysis by *objective, informed parties* who can identify limitations and force change — as the guiding principle of the whole program. |
| IV | **Model Development, Implementation, and Use** | The model card / inventory identity, intended use, components, and limitations. |
| **V** | **Model Validation** | The core `lub` surface. Its **three elements** (as prose, *not* lettered subsections): (1) evaluation of **conceptual soundness**, (2) **ongoing monitoring**, (3) **outcomes analysis**. |
| **VI** | **Governance, Policies, and Controls** | Board/senior-management roles, policies, roles & responsibilities, internal audit, **model inventory**, and **documentation**. |

> **Crosswalk-convention note.** `lub`'s machine-readable crosswalk
> (`src/lub/reports/crosswalk_data.toml`) assigns its own control-ids
> (`SR-11-7-V.A`, `-V.B`, `-VI.A/B/C`) and groups the validation elements into
> three named "pillars". **These control-id letters are `lub`'s own crosswalk
> convention, not verbatim OCC 2011-12 subsection citations** — SR 11-7 Section V
> is narrative prose with no lettered subsections, and *ongoing monitoring* is a
> Section V validation element (a v0.2 crosswalk revision will re-align the
> internal grouping; see `planning/32_SR117_Audit_Findings_2026-07-05.md`).

## How `lub` evidences each validation element (§V)

| SR 11-7 §V element | What the examiner wants | `lub` evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Conceptual soundness** | Predicted confidence is *meaningful* (predicted ≈ observed); comparison to alternatives. | Calibration metrics — `ece`, `rmsce`, `ence`, `brier`, `miscalibration_area`, `sharpness`, `spearman`, `kendall_tau`, `adversarial_group_calibration`. |
| **Ongoing monitoring** | Performance stays stable; drift is detected; the record is auditable over time. | `lub.calibration.drift`, adaptive-conformal coverage, `missing_ratio`, and the append-only `lub.ledger` (tamper-evident, replayable). |
| **Outcomes analysis** | Model outputs are compared to realized outcomes (back-testing); failure detection. | `accuracy`, `matthews_correlation`, `refusal_auroc`, `prr`, `reversed_pairs_proportion`, `aurc`, `auucc`. |

## Effective challenge (§III) — what `lub` does and does **not** do

Effective challenge is SR 11-7's **central principle**, and it is fundamentally a
**process performed by people independent of the model's developers**, with the
competence, incentive, and authority to escalate.

- **`lub` produces the evidence a challenger uses** — the continuous
  effective-challenge loop (`lub.challenge`: replay, reasoning-drift, and
  meta-calibration over aged predictions) surfaces where a model is weakening so
  an independent reviewer can act.
- **`lub` does NOT perform the independent review itself.** It does not supply
  the independent validator, and it cannot substitute for the human judgment,
  incentive structure, or organizational independence SR 11-7 requires.

## Governance, inventory & documentation (§VI)

- **Governed change control** (`lub.governance` + the Bridge console) enforces
  segregation of duties — propose → approve → apply by three different people —
  which maps to SR 11-7's controls-independent-of-development expectation.
- **Model inventory / documentation** — the model card pins identity, intended
  use, version/prompt/corpus fingerprints, and limitations.
- **Not covered by `lub`:** the enterprise model-inventory *register* across all
  models, board/senior-management oversight structure, and validation-staff
  independence — these are organizational, not library, responsibilities.

## Scope limit (read before citing)

`lub` supplies **quantitative calibration, performance, and provenance evidence**
that plugs into a model validation report. It does **not** replace: the
independent validation function, the qualitative review of model theory/design,
the enterprise model inventory, or the human effective challenge. Cite it as
*evidence for* an SR 11-7 program, not as *compliance with* SR 11-7.

## Why `lub` for SR 11-7

Unlike closed model-risk platforms (ValidMind, Calypso) that advertise SR 11-7
alignment, `lub`'s SR 11-7 evidence is **open-source, reproducible (seed +
dataset hash), and machine-readable (OSCAL)** — an auditor can re-run it and
verify every number, and a GRC platform can ingest the OSCAL Assessment Results
directly.
